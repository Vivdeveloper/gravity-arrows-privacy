Gravity Arrows – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Gravity Arrows is a puzzle game made for children. We designed it so that it does not collect personal information about the people who play it.

Information we collect

No personal information. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no way to enter personal details. It does send anonymous game statistics and crash reports, described below.

Information saved on the device

The game saves game progress (levels completed, stars, coins, chosen character and sound settings) only on the player's own device. This data never leaves the device and is deleted when the app is uninstalled. It can also be erased at any time from Settings → Reset progress.

Game statistics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

To find levels that are too hard and to fix bugs, the game uses two Google Firebase tools. They don't receive any name, email, photo or other personal details, and the game never uses or sends the Android advertising ID.

  • Google Analytics for Firebase records game events: which level was started or finished, stars, number of moves and hints used. It also receives basic technical details (app version, device model, Android version, approximate country from the IP address) and a random app-instance ID that is reset if the game is reinstalled. Advertising features, ad personalisation and advertising-ID collection are switched off.
  • Firebase Crashlytics sends a report when the game crashes: the error, the level being played, app version, device model and Android version, with a random installation ID.

This information is processed by Google on our behalf and is used only to improve the game. It is not sold, not shared for advertising and not used to contact or profile anyone. Analytics data is kept for up to 2 months and crash reports for up to 90 days. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Internet, ads and third parties

The game goes online only to send the statistics and crash reports above and to ask the Google Play Store app whether a newer version is available (if so, it asks the player to update). It contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, no links to websites or other apps, no social features and no tracking for advertising.

Reminder notifications

The game can show a friendly reminder to come back and play, at most every 3 hours between 9 am and 8 pm, stopping after 3 days without play. Reminders are created on the device itself; no information is sent anywhere to show them. They only appear if notifications are allowed, and can be turned off in Settings → Play reminders or in the phone's notification settings.

Permissions

The game only uses vibration (for gentle feedback, which can be turned off in Settings), audio playback for sound effects and music, and notifications for the optional reminders. It does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts or files.

Children's privacy

The game does not collect personal information from children. Statistics and crash reports are used only to keep the game working and improve it, never for advertising, in line with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR-K and Google Play's Families Policy.

Your choices

Because the data is not linked to a name or account, we can't look up an individual player. If you have questions or want us to delete data for a device, email us with the date and device model and we'll help.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
