Gravity Arrows – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Gravity Arrows is a puzzle game made for children. We designed it so that it does not collect any information about the people who play it.

Information we collect

None. The game does not collect, store, send or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no way to enter personal details.

Information saved on the device

The game saves game progress (levels completed, stars, coins, chosen character and sound settings) only on the player's own device. This data never leaves the device and is deleted when the app is uninstalled. It can also be erased at any time from Settings → Reset progress.

Internet, ads and third parties

The game does not connect to the internet while playing. It contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, no links to websites or other apps, no social features, and no analytics, tracking or third-party data-collection tools.

Permissions

The game only uses vibration (for gentle feedback, which can be turned off in Settings) and audio playback for sound effects and music. It does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts or files.

Children's privacy

Because no personal information is collected, the game complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR-K and Google Play's Families Policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
